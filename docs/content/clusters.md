## 5 strongest opportunities

1. **ontario als pcs / als pcs 5.4 / 5.5**: only government PDFs and base-hospital PDFs rank. No HTML explainer exists. An explainer with version dates is exactly what AI engines quote. Low difficulty, high fit (it's what our scenarios are built on).
2. **how to study for the aemca exam**: the top results are off-topic exam-tips pages and practice-test farms. Students are the app's users.
3. **pcp vs acp ontario**: answered only by service recruitment pages. Clear question, answer-first format, FAQ schema.
4. **stemi bypass ontario**: the provincial protocol is a 2015 PDF; nobody explains it plainly. Links into our live 12-lead and RV posts.
5. **scenario based training paramedics** (buyer): vendors are US/VR-centric. A neutral buyer's guide written for Ontario services is the page a training lead forwards to a chief.

## Competitors in the SERPs

- **Paramed Mastery** (iOS app, Ontario): 900+ flashcards, 230+ scenarios, ALS PCS 5.4. Closest product competitor for paramedic users.
- **ParaGuide** (directive reference app, Ontario).
- **PracticeTestGeeks, Examzify, Skool groups**: AEMCA practice-question farms.
- **Simulation Canada SIM Scenario Exchange**: free scenario library.
- **Laerdal SimCapture, VRpatients, SimTutor**: service-level simulation vendors.
- **Authorities that AI answers cite**: ontario.ca, rppeo.ca (OBHG), lhsc.on.ca (SWORBHP), cepcp.myobh.ca, corhealthontario.ca, Cambridge (CJEM), cpr.heart.org.
